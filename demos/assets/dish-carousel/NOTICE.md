# Bistro demo assets

Product photographs are original Bistro by Blinkit assets served by `cdn.grofers.com/da/cms-assets/cms/product/`. Okra and Retro font files originate from the Bistro Android application, version 2.49.1. These reference assets remain the property of their respective owners and are not covered by the component implementation's MIT license.

The demo reproduces guest-feed display copy and prices captured during development. It has no connection to stock, checkout, user accounts or Bistro's service.
